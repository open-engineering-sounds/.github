# Open Engineering Sounds

Engineering the complete lifecycle of sound for AI-native, open engineering systems.

Open Engineering Sounds is the home of reusable sound capabilities within the Open Engineering ecosystem.

Rather than treating audio as a collection of files, Open Engineering models sound as a first-class engineering discipline with semantic metadata, reusable capabilities, traceable workflows, and AI-driven automation.

Whether the source is a microphone, a film production, a robot, a live performance, or an AI-generated voice, Open Engineering Sounds provides a common foundation for capturing, processing, organizing, and delivering sound assets.

⸻

## Mission

Create an open, modular platform for engineering sound assets and sound-processing capabilities.

Open Engineering Sounds enables engineers, creators, robots, and AI agents to work with sound in a structured, reproducible, and composable way.

⸻

## Sound Separation

One of the core capabilities of this organization is Sound Separation.

A composite soundtrack can be transformed into reusable engineering assets such as:

* Dialogue
* Music
* Sound Effects

These derived assets can then be independently processed, localized, remixed, validated, and versioned while maintaining links to their original source and complete provenance.

Open Engineering models these outputs as derived engineering elements, preserving the relationship between the original recording and every generated artifact.
